# Audio integration

The existing combined Sound switch governs music, sound effects and native shooting-film audio. Sound starts off. AudioContext creation, decoding and playback begin after a user sound gesture. Neither loading nor playback controls a spin, accepted stake, result, preview return or settlement.

`dist/audio-pack.js` selects the prepared original music and seven effects in `dist/assets/audio/cashfire-2/`. The neutral `manifest.json` records hashes, decoded durations, sample rate, channels, measured loudness, true peak and event roles. `NOTICE.txt` and `docs/audio-notice.md` restrict audio to the integrated personal prototype; code/art access grants no standalone audio reuse. Raw exports, probes and private generation/account records stay outside this source distribution. The Journey Art Kit excludes every audio format and sample archive, while retaining mapping and notices.

Music is a 31.9-second tactical funk bed; spin is a 2.24-second mechanical loop. Prepared boundary crossfades are retained without re-encoding. FFmpeg decoding measured 1,531,200 and 107,520 stereo frames at 48 kHz respectively. Explicit loop regions avoid padding outside those periods, and resume offsets wrap within the same region. PCM continuity measurements show no silence gap at either boundary; these measurements cannot establish perceived musical phrasing or browser decoder behavior without listening.

The music gain is 0.72. Motor, reel-stop and the four crate accents use measured gains from the manifest/configuration. A landed crate has one accent owner; the collection event is silent for this shared-cue mapping so it does not echo the same hit. Normal and Mega each have their own single entry sting. Ready footage may truncate the entry sting because native playback has priority; audio completion never delays the film. Return from reveal uses one transition cue. Only a confirmed positive net result permits the win accent; a free preview does not claim a paid win. Compatibility baseline cues remain for the original fallback map and interface tick.

Music, gameplay effects, feature entry and UI have separate buses. Loading, film playback, pause and media error suppress the base bed/effects and cancel stale voices, giving the original native AAC priority. Reveal permits the existing subdued music bed and applicable result cue. Return restores the base mix with a bounded transition. Repeated preview/Skip/Return, mute and hidden-tab cycles retain one music loop and one motor at most. Short source fades and bus recovery do not alter gameplay timing.

Selected URLs fetch/decode once, including the shared crate-hit aliases. Missing or failed audio remains non-blocking; fallback synthesis and mute state follow the same phase policy. Numeric gains, loops and voice limits are defensive and bounded. Automated tests cover gesture gating, mapping, configured gains and loop offsets, repeated state changes, voice cancellation, mute/pause, stale/late loads, native-film suppression and offline fallback.

No local browser, headphone or phone listening was available during this integration. Timbre, perceived loop seams, full-round synchronization, speaker/mono balance and physical-device performance remain listening/device checks. Format, loudness, PCM and VM evidence are recorded separately in `docs/review/`.
